# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Be respectful, specific, and constructive. Discuss behavior and evidence, not personal characteristics. Protect the privacy of users, maintainers, and organizations when sharing examples or debugging information.

Harassment, threats, discrimination, doxxing, deliberate disclosure of private data, and repeated bad-faith disruption are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

For ordinary conduct concerns, contact the maintainer through a private repository channel. For security or sensitive-data exposure, use a GitHub Security Advisory. Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove content and restrict participation when needed to protect the project and its community.
